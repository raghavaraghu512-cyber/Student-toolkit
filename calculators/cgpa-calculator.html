Calculate CGPA

Formula

CGPA = Σ(Grade Point × Credit) ÷ Σ Credits

Example

Subject 1 → Grade = 9, Credit = 4

Subject 2 → Grade = 8, Credit = 3

Subject 3 → Grade = 10, Credit = 4

CGPA = (9×4 + 8×3 + 10×4) ÷ (4+3+4) = 9.09
